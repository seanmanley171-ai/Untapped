UNTAPPED — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

UNTAPPED is published by Manley Inc. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens with your information when you use the UNTAPPED app and website.

The short version

UNTAPPED collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

What you enter stays on your device

The resources, abilities, goals, projects and progress you add are stored only in the app's storage on your own device or browser. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or processes this information, and we cannot see it.

Data sharing

Because nothing is collected, nothing is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.

Deleting your data

You can delete any item inside the app at any time. Removing the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes everything the app stored.

Children

UNTAPPED is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
