UNTAPPED — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

UNTAPPED is published by Manley Inc. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens with your information when you use the UNTAPPED app and website.

The short version

UNTAPPED requires an account. We store your email address and the app data you enter so the app can work and your data can follow you between devices. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your account

To use UNTAPPED you create an account with an email address and a password. Accounts and app data are stored with our backend provider, Supabase. Your password is stored by the auth provider in hashed form — we cannot see it.

What you enter

The resources, abilities, goals, projects and progress you add are saved to your account in Supabase, and also kept in the app's storage on your own device so the app can open fast and work offline. Only your account can read your data. We do not share it with other users.

Subscriptions and payments

UNTAPPED is a subscription app at $4.99/month. Subscription payments are handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card or payment details. We receive a subscription status (for example, active or canceled) and a purchase token from Google Play so the app knows whether your subscription is current.

How data travels

Data between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. It is processed only by the services needed to run the app: Supabase (accounts and stored app data) and Google Play (subscription payments).

Deleting your data

You can delete any item inside the app at any time. To delete your whole account and its stored data, email us at seanmanley171@gmail.com from your account email and ask for account deletion.

Children

UNTAPPED is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or account deletion: email seanmanley171@gmail.com.
